# ACET

ACET is a trust-focused professional ecosystem built to help people and businesses make better decisions through verification, evidence, and intelligent recommendations.

## About ACET

The professional and employment ecosystem can be difficult to navigate. Professionals encounter fake opportunities, unclear recruiters, and uncertainty about whether an opportunity is legitimate or a good fit. Businesses also face challenges identifying professionals whose skills and experience are supported by real evidence.

ACET aims to bring greater trust and transparency into this ecosystem.

Its core philosophy is:



ACET focuses on:
 Trust and verification
 Professional evidence
 Intelligent recommendations
 Better opportunity matching
 Trusted connections between professionals and businesses
